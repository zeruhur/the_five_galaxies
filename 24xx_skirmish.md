# 24XX - SKIRMISH

## RULES

### OBJECTIVE

Players control small squads of specialists, battling over objectives. Victory conditions depend on the mission: eliminating enemies, securing locations, or completing objectives.

### TURN STRUCTURE

* Both players roll a d20.  
* The higher roll chooses to go first or second.  
* Players alternate activating one unit at a time.  
* On a unit's turn, they can take a Move and 1 Actions:  
  * Attack (shoot or melee)  
  * Use an Ability (heal, hack, etc.)  
  * Interact (open doors, activate objectives, reload, etc.)  
* A unit can sacrifice a Move action for an extra action.

### MOVEMENT & TERRAIN

* Standard movement: 6 inches (or 1 zone) per action.  
* Difficult terrain (rubble, water, etc.) halves movement (3 inches).  
* Climbing requires a Skill Check.

## COMBAT

### ATTACKING

* Choose a target within range and roll a skill die:  
  * d6 if skilled (Shooting, Hand-to-hand, etc.).  
  * d4 if hindered (injury, cover, etc.).  
  * d8 or higher if advanced training applies.  
* Compare the result:  
  * 1-2 (Miss) – No effect.  
  * 3-4 (Grazing Hit) – Deals 1 damage.  
  * 5+ (Solid Hit) – Deals 2 damage.

### RANGED COMBAT

* Each weapon has a Range and Damage profile (see Weapons Table).  
* Targets behind Cover force the attacker to roll one die size lower (d6 → d4).  
* Overwatch: If a unit skips their turn, they can fire once per enemy movement within range.

### MELEE COMBAT

* If a unit is adjacent to an enemy, they can use Hand-to-hand combat instead of shooting.  
* Melee ignores cover and forces both combatants to roll their melee skill simultaneously.  
* The highest roller hits first, dealing damage.

## DEFENSE, ARMOR & HEALTH

### HEALTH & DAMAGE

* Standard units have 3 HP. Tougher units (Muscle, Androids) may have 4 HP.  
* If a unit reaches 0 HP, they are removed from play.

### ARMOR & SHIELDS

* Light Armor (vest) – Absorbs 1 hit, then breaks.  
* Heavy Armor (battle armor, hardsuit) – Absorbs 3 hits before breaking.  
* Shields – Some units have energy shields that absorb 1-2 hits before failing.

## SPECIAL RULES

### REINFORCEMENTS

In longer games, fallen units may return after 2 rounds with basic gear and 1 HP.

### ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS

* Exploding Barrels: Any hit deals 2 damage in a small area.  
* Low Gravity: Movement is doubled, but shooting rolls at \-1 die size.  
* Security Systems: Auto-turrets, cameras, or laser grids force Stealth or Tech checks to bypass.

## OBJECTIVES & SCENARIOS

Each game lasts 3-5 rounds and uses one of the following missions:

| Mission | Objective | Win Condition |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Capture the Point | Hold a central point | Control it for 2 rounds |
| Elimination | Wipe out enemy forces | Last squad standing |
| Data Heist | Hack terminals while fighting off enemies | First to hack 2 terminals |
| Escort | Guide a VIP to an exit | Reach the safe zone with the VIP |

## SQUAD BUILDING

* Players get 15 points to build their squad.  
* A squad consists of 3-6 units, each with skills, gear, and weapons.

# CAMPAIGNS & ADVENTURES

## OVERVIEW

Transform 24XX Skirmish into an ongoing narrative campaign where your crew takes on jobs, builds relationships, and shapes their destiny in the cyberpunk future. Each battle is part of a larger story driven by random events, character development, and player choices.

## CAMPAIGN SETUP

### YOUR CREW

Instead of building squads for individual battles, you maintain a persistent crew:

* **Starting Crew**: 4 characters with 12 points total (3 points each)  
* **Crew Limit**: Maximum 8 active characters  
* **Reserves**: Unlimited inactive characters (injured, in jail, etc.)

### CREW ROLES

Each character should have a defined role in your operation:

**Field Roles** (go on missions):

* **Leader**: Face or Muscle who makes key decisions  
* **Specialist**: Tech, Medic, or Psychic with unique abilities  
* **Muscle**: Combat-focused characters  
* **Scout**: Sneak specialists for reconnaissance

**Support Roles** (help between missions):

* **Fixer**: Generates job opportunities  
* **Medic**: Speeds healing and reduces injury severity  
* **Fence**: Improves equipment acquisition and selling  
* **Hacker**: Provides intel and electronic warfare support

### CREW SHIP & BASE

Your crew operates from a mobile base:

* **Ship Components**: Life Support, Navigation, Cargo Hold, Crew Quarters  
* **Upgrades**: Medical Bay (+1 to healing), Workshop (repair gear), Armory (store weapons)  
* **Debt**: Start with 2d6 Credits owed to various parties

## CAMPAIGN TURN SEQUENCE

Each campaign turn represents roughly one week and follows these phases:

### 1\. UPKEEP PHASE

* **Pay Expenses**: 1 Credit per active crew member  
* **Heal Injuries**: Make healing rolls for wounded characters  
* **Advance Storylines**: Check ongoing plot threads

### 2\. CREW ACTIONS PHASE

Choose one action per character not going on the mission:

* **Find Work**: Roll for job opportunities  
* **Trade**: Buy/sell equipment  
* **Train**: Improve skills or learn new ones  
* **Recruit**: Find new crew members  
* **Investigate**: Research plot hooks or enemies  
* **Lay Low**: Reduce Heat with authorities

### 3\. TRAVEL PHASE

* **Choose Destination**: Select world/sector to visit  
* **Travel Events**: Roll for encounters during travel  
* **Arrival**: Learn about local situation and opportunities

### 4\. MISSION PHASE

* **Select Mission**: Choose from available jobs  
* **Pre-Mission**: Gather intel, plan approach  
* **Battle**: Play out the skirmish using core rules  
* **Post-Mission**: Handle rewards, consequences, and story developments

### 5\. POST-MISSION PHASE

* **Experience**: Award XP based on performance  
* **Reputation**: Adjust standing with various factions  
* **Story Hooks**: Generate new plot threads  
* **Heat**: Check for law enforcement attention

## CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT

### EXPERIENCE SYSTEM

Characters earn XP individually and as a crew:

**Individual XP Sources:**

* Survive a mission: 1 XP  
* Achieve personal objective: 1 XP  
* Perform heroic action: 1 XP  
* Suffer major setback: 1 XP

**Crew XP Sources:**

* Complete mission successfully: 2 XP (split among participants)  
* Exceptional success: \+1 bonus XP  
* Advance major story arc: 3 XP (split among all)

**Spending XP:**

* **Skill Improvement** (5 XP): Increase die size (d4→d6→d8→d10→d12)  
* **New Skill** (3 XP): Learn skill at d4 level  
* **Cybernetics** (4 XP): Gain permanent enhancement  
* **Contacts** (2 XP): Develop relationship with NPC  
* **Ship Upgrade** (Variable): Improve crew capabilities

### ADVANCEMENT TRACKS

Characters can specialize further as they gain experience:

**Combat Veteran** (Muscle progression):

* **Rank 1**: \+1 HP, ignore first injury per mission  
* **Rank 2**: Attack twice per action with different targets  
* **Rank 3**: Inspire nearby allies (+1 die size for 1 turn)

**Tech Specialist** (Tech progression):

* **Rank 1**: Hack at range, bypass one security system per mission  
* **Rank 2**: Control enemy equipment for 1 turn  
* **Rank 3**: Upload virus that spreads between connected systems

**Shadow Operative** (Sneak progression):

* **Rank 1**: Invisible until attacking, \+3" movement  
* **Rank 2**: Backstab for double damage  
* **Rank 3**: Vanish mid-combat (remove from board, return anywhere next turn)

## FACTION SYSTEM

### MAJOR FACTIONS

Track your standing (-3 to \+3) with key groups:

**Mega-Corporations**: Control most of civilized space

* **Allied**: Steady high-paying jobs, legal protection  
* **Enemy**: Corporate assassins, legal troubles, restricted access

**Criminal Syndicates**: Control illegal activities

* **Allied**: Underground contacts, illegal goods, safe havens  
* **Enemy**: Gang wars, territory disputes, assassination attempts

**Law Enforcement**: Government and corporate security

* **Allied**: Legal immunity, inside information, official backing  
* **Enemy**: Constant harassment, arrest warrants, bounty hunters

**Rebel Groups**: Fight corporate oppression

* **Allied**: Idealistic allies, underground railroad, cause bonuses  
* **Enemy**: Terrorist accusations, corporate bounties, moral conflicts

### FACTION EVENTS

Each turn, roll d6. On 6+, roll faction event:

**Corporate Event (d6):**

1. Merger creates new opportunities  
2. Executive assassination changes power structure  
3. New product launch requires testing/protection  
4. Corporate war spills into your territory  
5. Whistleblower needs protection  
6. Boardroom coup creates chaos

**Criminal Event (d6):**

1. Territory war erupts  
2. New drug/tech hits streets  
3. Gang leader imprisoned/killed  
4. Rival crew encroaches on your territory  
5. Big score opportunity emerges  
6. Police crackdown begins

## ADVANCED CAMPAIGN RULES

### HEAT & LAW ENFORCEMENT

Track Heat level (0-10):

**Gaining Heat:**

* Failed stealth: \+1  
* Civilian casualties: \+2  
* Corporate facility attack: \+3  
* Public violence: \+1

**Heat Effects:**

* **1-3**: Occasional patrols  
* **4-6**: Active investigation  
* **7-9**: Manhunt in progress  
* **10+**: Military response

**Reducing Heat:**

* Lay Low action: \-1  
* Frame someone else: \-2  
* Pay bribes: \-1 per Credit spent  
* Change identities: \-3 (expensive)

### CREW MORALE

Track crew satisfaction (1-10):

**High Morale (8-10)**: Bonus XP, loyalty bonuses **Average Morale (4-7)**: Normal operations  
**Low Morale (1-3)**: Risk of desertion, poor performance

**Morale Factors:**

* Recent victories: \+1  
* Good pay: \+1  
* Crew deaths: \-2  
* Failed missions: \-1  
* Personal conflicts: \-1

### SHIP OPERATIONS

Your ship is crucial for campaign operations:

**Ship Status:**

* **Pristine**: All systems optimal  
* **Functional**: Normal operations  
* **Damaged**: \-1 to all ship actions  
* **Critical**: Risk of breakdown

**Ship Actions:**

* **High-Speed Run**: Escape pursuit, \+1 Heat  
* **Deep Space**: Avoid authorities, limited supplies  
* **Luxury Travel**: \+1 Morale, expensive  
* **Cargo Run**: Earn Credits, delayed arrival

## CAMPAIGN VICTORY CONDITIONS

### CREW GOALS

Set long-term objectives for meaningful progression:

**Knowledge**: Uncover major conspiracy or ancient secret **Revenge**: Defeat specific nemesis faction or individual **Legacy**: Establish lasting change in the universe

# SOLO RULES

These solo rules let you play 24XX Skirmish against an AI opponent controlled by dice and simple decision trees. The AI uses aggressive tactics and adapts to different mission types.

## SETUP

### SQUAD COMPOSITION

* **Your Squad**: Build normally with 15 points  
* **AI Squad**: Use pre-built squads or roll randomly:  
  * Roll d6 for squad type: 1-2 Assault, 3-4 Balanced, 5-6 Specialist  
  * AI always gets 15 points worth of units

### AI SQUAD TEMPLATES

**ASSAULT SQUAD (15 PTS)**

* 2x Muscle with Rifles (8 pts)  
* 1x Medic with SMG (5 pts)  
* 1x Face with Shotgun (2 pts)

**BALANCED SQUAD (15 PTS)**

* 1x Muscle with Rifle (5 pts)  
* 1x Tech with SMG (4 pts)  
* 1x Sneak with Pistol (2 pts)  
* 1x Medic with Pistol (3 pts)  
* 1x Face with Pistol (2 pts)

**SPECIALIST SQUAD (15 PTS)**

* 1x Psychic with Rifle (5 pts)  
* 1x Tech with Hardsuit (5 pts)  
* 1x Sneak with Jetpack (5 pts)

## AI TURN SEQUENCE

### 1\. PRIORITY DETERMINATION

Roll d6 to determine AI's tactical priority this turn:

* **1-2: Aggressive** \- Focus on attacking  
* **3-4: Objective** \- Focus on mission goals  
* **5-6: Tactical** \- Focus on positioning/support

### 2\. UNIT ACTIVATION ORDER

AI activates units based on priority:

* **Aggressive**: Wounded units first, then highest damage dealers  
* **Objective**: Units closest to objectives first  
* **Tactical**: Support units first, then combat units

### 3\. AI UNIT ACTIONS

For each AI unit, follow this decision tree:

#### STEP 1: TARGET SELECTION

1. **Primary Targets**: Wounded player units in range  
2. **Secondary Targets**: Closest player unit in range  
3. **No Targets**: Move toward closest player unit or objective

#### STEP 2: ACTION SELECTION

Roll d6 and modify based on situation:

**COMBAT ACTIONS (+2 IF AGGRESSIVE PRIORITY)**

* **1-3**: Move \+ Attack if target in range after move  
* **4-5**: Attack \+ Move (if already in range)  
* **6+**: Use special ability \+ attack (if available)

**OBJECTIVE ACTIONS (+2 IF OBJECTIVE PRIORITY)**

* **1-2**: Move toward objective  
* **3-4**: Interact with objective if adjacent  
* **5-6**: Attack then move to objective

**SUPPORT ACTIONS (+2 IF TACTICAL PRIORITY)**

* **1-3**: Move to cover/support position  
* **4-5**: Use healing/support ability  
* **6+**: Overwatch

## AI BEHAVIOR RULES

### MOVEMENT PRIORITIES

1. **Wounded Units**: Always seek cover  
2. **Ranged Units**: Maintain distance, use cover  
3. **Melee Units**: Close distance aggressively  
4. **Support Units**: Stay behind combat units

### TARGET SELECTION

AI always chooses targets in this order:

1. Wounded player units they can kill this turn  
2. Player units threatening AI objectives  
3. Closest player unit in range  
4. Highest threat player unit (Muscle, then Psychic, then others)

### SPECIAL ABILITIES

* **Medics**: Heal wounded AI units within 6"  
* **Psychics**: Use abilities on player units within 12"  
* **Techs**: Hack objectives or disrupt player equipment  
* **Face**: Use abilities to support other AI units

## MISSION SPECIFIC AI BEHAVIOR

### CAPTURE THE POINT

* **Turn 1-2**: Rush to central point  
* **Turn 3+**: Defend point, attack approaching players  
* AI wins if they control point for 2 consecutive rounds

### ELIMINATION

* **Always**: Focus on eliminating wounded player units  
* **Defensive**: Pull back wounded AI units  
* AI wins by eliminating all player units

### DATA HEIST

* **Priority**: Send Tech/Face units to terminals  
* **Secondary**: Other units provide cover fire  
* AI wins by hacking 2 terminals first

### ESCORT

* **AI Goal**: Eliminate the VIP or block escape route  
* **Tactics**: Ambush tactics, focus fire on VIP  
* AI wins if VIP is eliminated or blocked for 5 rounds

## AI DIFFICULTY SCALING

### EASY MODE

* AI rolls one die size smaller for all attacks (d6→d4)  
* AI gets \-1 to priority rolls  
* Player wins ties in activation order

### NORMAL MODE

* Use rules as written

### HARD MODE

* AI gets \+1 HP per unit  
* AI can activate 2 units simultaneously once per turn  
* AI gets \+1 to all skill rolls

### NIGHTMARE MODE

* AI squad costs 18 points instead of 15  
* AI always wins initiative ties  
* Environmental hazards favor the AI

## RANDOM EVENTS

Roll d6 at the start of each round after Round 1:

**1\. Reinforcements** AI gets 1 additional unit (roll random type, basic gear)

**2\. Equipment Malfunction** Random player unit's primary weapon jams (skip next attack)

**3\. Environmental Shift** Change lighting, gravity, or add hazard to center of map

**4\. Intel Breakthrough** Player gains \+1 to next skill roll of choice

**5\. Tactical Advantage** AI gets extra activation this round

**6\. No Event** Continue as normal

# ADDENDUM: SWITCHING FROM 24XX WORLD PATCH

Play WORLD PATCH as your default. Drop into Skirmish when a fight needs positions and turns. Return to WORLD PATCH once it's resolved.

## WHEN TO SWITCH

Not every fight needs Skirmish. Most opposition resolves fine under CONFLICT. Switch only when a fight's shape matches a Skirmish mission: Capture the Point, Elimination, Data Heist, Escort. If it doesn't fit one, resolve it in WORLD PATCH instead.

One WORLD PATCH conflict scene equals one Skirmish battle, 3 to 5 rounds. Enter, fight it out, return. No switching mid-scene.

## CHARACTER CONVERSION

Skill dice carry over as is.

**No skill**: roll d6, or d4 if hindered.
**d8, d10, or d12**: roll that die. Treat d12 as advanced training.
**Gear**: carries over by name. Don't re-spend Skirmish's squad points on a character already in play, those points balance a squad built from scratch.

## OPPOSITION CONVERSION

WORLD PATCH opponents carry no dice, only behavior, risk, and obstacle. Skirmish wants a stat block. Convert on the fly:

| WORLD PATCH opposition | Skirmish stat block |
| :--- | :--- |
| Minor obstacle, low risk | 2 HP, no armor |
| Dangerous risk, no real obstacle | 3 HP, no armor, hits hard |
| Obstacle and risk both present | 3-4 HP, light armor, 1 hit absorbed |
| Heavily obstacled or armored threat | 4 HP, heavy armor, up to 3 hits absorbed |

Keep the enemy's established behavior as the AI's priority. Don't default to Aggressive just because a fight started.

## CONDITION CONVERSION

Skirmish tracks flat HP and breakable armor, not a percentage. Read a character's state in four tiers.

**Unhurt**: full HP, armor intact.
**Grazed**: HP down, armor holding or broken once.
**Wounded**: last HP, or hindered, roll d4.
**Down**: 0 HP, removed from the board.

## NARRATIVE TO TACTICAL

**Scene**: pick the Skirmish mission from the scene's stakes. A rescue becomes Escort, a heist becomes Data Heist.
**Stakes**: whatever was on the line in the scene, a hostage, a deadline, a piece of intel, carries forward as the Skirmish win condition. Not a separate reward.

## RETURNING TO NARRATIVE

**Condition**: read each tier back as HARM. Unhurt needs nothing. Grazed and Wounded are ordinary injuries needing time or medical attention. Down is a death risk, not an automatic kill. Favor inclusion over realism, as the core rules already ask.
**Performance**: fold good tactics into the GM's judgment when handing out WORLD PATCH's single advancement, one skill increase and d6 credits. Don't run a second XP track.
**Result**: let victory or defeat resolve the scene's fictional question and feed back into whatever WORLD PATCH was tracking, reputation, a patron, the next story hook.
